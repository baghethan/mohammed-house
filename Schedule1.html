xl: 0.75rem full: 9999px spacing: base: 8px xs: 4px sm: 12px md: 16px lg: 24px xl: 32px grid-margin: 24px grid-gutter: 16px --- ## Brand & Style The design system is engineered for the high-stakes environment of automotive enterprise management. It prioritizes **Reliability, Operational Excellence, and Clarity**. The visual language is rooted in the **Corporate / Modern** style, emphasizing a structured hierarchy that reduces cognitive load for employees managing complex logistics and personnel data. The system utilizes a refined aesthetic that balances the heritage of Almajdouie Motors with a forward-looking digital workspace. The interface focuses on high legibility and efficient data density, ensuring that administrative tasks feel precise and effortless. The emotional response is one of stability and trust, achieved through a disciplined use of whitespace and a sober, professional color palette. ## Colors The color strategy for the design system is anchored by a deep corporate blue, signifying authority and institutional strength. This primary blue is used for key touchpoints such as primary actions, navigation headers, and active states. A light gray and white foundation provides a clean, "breathable" environment, preventing the data-heavy interface from feeling cramped. For functional feedback, the system employs a high-visibility semantic palette: - **Success Green:** Used for "Off" status and completed tasks. - **Warning Amber:** Used for "Annual Leave" and pending approvals. - **Danger Red:** Used for urgent alerts and critical errors. - **Corporate Blue (Primary):** Used for "Morning" and "Night" shift indicators to maintain a professional brand presence within the schedule. ## Typography The design system utilizes **Inter** for both English and Arabic interfaces. Inter’s tall x-height and neutral character make it exceptionally legible for data tables and dense information displays. For Arabic implementation, line heights are increased by 20% to accommodate the script's ascending and descending characters without crowding the baseline. Headlines are kept bold and concise, while body text prioritizes a comfortable reading rhythm. Label styles are used for status badges and table headers, employing a slightly heavier weight to distinguish them from interactive data. ## Layout & Spacing The layout philosophy follows a **Fluid Grid** model with a standard 12-column architecture. This ensures the employee management system remains functional across desktop monitors and tablets used in the field. The system relies on an 8px spacing scale (8, 16, 24, 32, etc.) to create a consistent vertical rhythm. Data tables are designed with "Comfortable," "Standard," and "Compact" density settings, allowing users to control the amount of information visible on-screen. Sidebars are fixed at 280px for primary navigation, while content areas expand fluidly to fill the viewport. ## Elevation & Depth To maintain a professional and trustworthy appearance, the design system avoids heavy shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** and **Low-contrast Outlines**. Depth is communicated through surface color shifts rather than physical metaphors. 1. **Level 0 (Base):** The primary background color (Light Gray), used for the application canvas. 2. **Level 1 (Surface):** White containers for cards and tables, featuring a subtle 1px border (#E2E8F0) to define boundaries. 3. **Level 2 (Overlay):** Used for dropdowns and date pickers. These elements use a soft, highly diffused ambient shadow (10% opacity, 12px blur) to appear slightly lifted above the main interface. 4. **Active States:** Interactive elements like buttons use a slight inner shadow or color deepening on press to provide tactile feedback without breaking the flat corporate aesthetic. ## Shapes The design system adopts a **Soft** shape language. A standard 4px (0.25rem) radius is applied to most UI components, including input fields, buttons, and table rows. This subtle rounding softens the industrial nature of the data while maintaining the rigid structure required for an enterprise tool. Larger containers, such as dashboard cards and the navigation sidebar, utilize an 8px (0.5rem) radius. Status badges for shift management (Morning, Night, etc.) use a fully rounded "pill" shape to distinguish them clearly from interactive buttons and static labels. ## Components ### Data Tables Tables are the core of this design system. They feature sticky headers, sortable columns, and zebra-striping for readability. Row hover states use a light blue tint to help users track information horizontally. ### Status Badges Badges are used to communicate shift status and availability at a glance: - **Morning:** Primary Blue background, white text. - **Night:** Deep Navy background, white text. - **Off:** Success Green background, white text. - **Annual Leave:** Warning Amber background, dark text. ### Navigation Sidebar A clear, vertical navigation bar on the left features high-contrast icons and clear labels. The active state is indicated by a thick vertical bar in the primary corporate blue on the leading edge of the menu item. ### Date Pickers The date picker is designed for speed, allowing for quick month/year jumps. It utilizes a grid-based calendar view with the primary corporate blue used for selected dates and subtle gray circles for the current date. ### Input Fields & Controls Form elements use a standard 40px height for optimal touch and click targets. Borders are neutral gray, turning primary blue on focus. Error states are clearly marked with a red border and supporting helper text.
